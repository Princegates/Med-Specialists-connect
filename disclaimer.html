Who we are and what we do

MEDs Specialists Connect is a healthcare operations and concierge company. We help people find and reach specialists and services, we coordinate care, and we manage specialized medical units in partnership with clinics. We are not a hospital, an emergency service or an insurer.

This website is not medical advice

Information on this website is general. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always speak to a qualified health professional about your health, and never ignore or delay medical advice because of something you read here.

In an emergency

If you or someone else is in danger or needs urgent medical care, call your nearest emergency service or go to the nearest hospital straight away. Do not use the request form, the referral form or WhatsApp for emergencies.

Care is provided by others

The specialists, clinics and other providers we connect you with are responsible for the care and advice they give. Where we manage a unit inside a clinic, clinical decisions are made by the clinicians responsible for your care.

Appointments and availability

Sending a request does not guarantee an appointment or a particular specialist. Availability depends on the provider.

Fees

Providers set their own fees for consultations, tests, procedures and other services. Please ask us about any fee that applies before you agree to a service.

Insurance

We help with insurance coordination and claims follow-up. We are not an insurer, and we cannot promise that a claim will be approved or paid.

Using this website

Please give accurate information when you contact us. We may not be able to help if details are missing or wrong. Links to other websites and services, such as WhatsApp and maps, are controlled by their owners.

Changes and contact

We may update this page, and the date at the top shows when it last changed. Questions? Email medspecialistsconnect@gmail.com or call 0243 560 080. Your personal information is covered in our privacy notice.
