About our private and home-based services

Our private and home-based healthcare services are delivered by appropriately qualified and registered healthcare professionals, in line with applicable professional standards.

Your invoice lists what you are paying for:

  • The consultation and assessment by the relevant medical specialist.
  • Nursing care, shown as its own line on your invoice.
  • Blood sample collection by a qualified laboratory technician, where necessary, shown as its own line on your invoice.

Laboratory tests, follow-up care and any other service your doctor recommends are arranged with you and charged separately. We tell you what applies before you pay.

Our goal is to provide convenient, safe and professional healthcare while maintaining the highest standards of patient care and confidentiality.

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need a referral from a doctor?

No. You can tell us what you need, or which specialist you want to see. If a doctor has already referred you, tell us and we will work from that.

What happens after I send a request?

Our team calls you back to understand what you need. We then find an appropriate specialist or service and arrange the next steps, at a clinic or at home where possible.

How much does it cost, and how do I pay?

Each service has a clear charge, and our coordination fee is shown separately from what doctors, hospitals and laboratories charge. Fixed fees are invoiced straight away. Where a provider’s price varies, we send an estimate first and you pay once the details are confirmed. See how each service is charged.

What should I do in an emergency?

Call your nearest emergency service or go straight to the nearest hospital. Please do not use the request form or WhatsApp for emergencies.

Who provides my medical care?

The specialists, clinics and other providers we connect you with are responsible for the care they give. MEDs Specialists Connect coordinates your care and manages the operations around it. Read our medical disclaimer.

Are you an insurance company?

No. We help with insurance coordination and claims follow-up, but we are not an insurer and cannot promise that a claim will be approved or paid.

Is my information kept private?

We use your details only to deal with your request, and we do not sell them. Please read the privacy notice for the full picture.

I am a hospital or clinic. How do we work with you?

We partner with specialist clinics and healthcare facilities to establish, manage and operate specialized medical units. Get in touch and tell us what you have in mind.
