For patients and families

Your virtual hospital front desk.

Call us or use this website, describe what you need and we walk you through every step, from your home or wherever you are.

  • Specialist appointments

    Referred by your doctor, or looking for one? We find an appropriate private specialist and arrange your consultation.

  • Insurance assistance

    Help with insurance coordination and claims follow-up so you are not left chasing paperwork.

  • Diagnostics

    We coordinate tests and scans with diagnostic centres and help you get the results to the right doctor.

  • Care at home

    Arrangements for a doctor or nurse to see your loved one at home, with the hospital details taken care of.

  • Ambulance coordination

    We help arrange ambulance transport between home and hospital when you need it.

  • Advice on where to go

    Not sure which specialist or facility you need? Call us and we will point you in the right direction.

How it works

From “I need help” to seen by a specialist.

  1. Step 1

    Tell us

    Call us or send a request on this website. Describe what you need or which specialist you want to see.

  2. Step 2

    We match you

    We find an appropriate private specialist or service for your situation.

  3. Step 3

    We arrange it

    We schedule the appointment, at a clinic or from the comfort of your home where possible.

  4. Step 4

    We follow through

    Insurance, diagnostics and logistics are coordinated so your care stays on track.

Our company

Bridging medical expertise and efficient service.

MEDs Specialist Connect is a healthcare operations and medical services management company that partners with specialist clinics and healthcare facilities to establish, manage, and operate specialized medical units.

We provide end-to-end operational support, including the deployment and management of medical equipment, administrative services, insurance coordination, logistics, and day-to-day clinical unit operations.

Our mission

To provide patients with a seamless, turnkey medical experience through efficient operations, integrated care, and a strong commitment to quality healthcare.

Our commitment

To bridge the gap between medical expertise, healthcare infrastructure, technology, and efficient service management, helping healthcare institutions expand their specialized service offerings while delivering accessible, coordinated, and high-quality patient care.
