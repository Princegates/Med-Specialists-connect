Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information MEDs Specialists Connect (“we”) collects through this website, why, and what we do with it. Our address is 6 Worship Curve, Spintex Road, and you can reach us at info@medspecialistconnect.com or on 0243 560 080.

What we collect

When you send a request, a referral or a message, we collect the details you type in. Depending on the form, this can be your name, phone number, what you need, who it is for, how urgent it is, the best time to call, your area, your organisation, the specialty needed, and any note you add. It can include information about health.

Please share only what we need to understand your request, and do not send full medical records through the forms. If you call, email or message us on WhatsApp, we also receive what you share there.

How we use it

  • To call you back, understand what you need, and arrange or coordinate the service you asked for, such as a specialist appointment, diagnostics, home care or insurance help.
  • To deal with referrals from doctors and enquiries from hospitals and clinics.

We do not use your details for advertising and we do not sell them.

Who can see it

Our team can see it. With your agreement, the specialists, clinics, diagnostic centres and other providers we arrange care with can see what they need to help you. We may also share information where the law requires it.

Services that help us run this website

  • Form delivery. Form submissions are sent to us through Web3Forms, a form-delivery service, which passes them to our email inbox.
  • Payments. If you pay an invoice online, Paystack processes the payment. We never see your card or Mobile Money PIN.
  • Email. A reputable email service sends our invoices, receipts and messages to you.
  • Hosting. Cloudflare hosts this website and, like any web host, processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver and protect the site.
  • WhatsApp and maps. If you tap a WhatsApp or map link, that service handles your chat or search under its own terms.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It remembers your day or night display choice on your own device, and nothing else.

How long we keep it

We keep your details only for as long as we need them to deal with your request and to meet our legal and professional obligations. After that we delete them or remove the details that identify you.

Keeping it safe

Your health information is treated as sensitive. This is what we do to protect it:

  • Encrypted in transit. Everything you send to this website travels over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • Only the people who need it. Our staff sign in with their own account and a second step (an authenticator code). What each person can see depends on their role, and people who do not need health details do not see them.
  • A record of access. We keep a log of who opened or changed a record. Staff are signed out automatically when they stop using the system.
  • Minimal emails. Our internal emails about a new request give the case number and the service, not your name, phone number or health note.
  • Private links. Your invoice and tracking links cannot be guessed, show no health details, and are not stored on shared devices by this website.
  • If something goes wrong. If your information is ever lost or seen by someone who should not have it, we will investigate straight away, tell you and the Data Protection Commission as the law requires, and tell you what we are doing about it.

No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, which is why we ask you not to send full medical records through the forms. We collect what we need from you directly.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or delete it, and withdraw your agreement to be contacted at any time. Email us at info@medspecialistconnect.com or call 0243 560 080.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
