Privacy Policy

MEDs Specialists Connect®

Effective date: 24 November 2026
Last updated: 24 November 2026

1. Introduction

MEDs Specialists Connect® (“MEDs”, “we”, “our” or “us”) is committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality and security of the personal information entrusted to us by our clients, patients, healthcare professionals, business partners and website visitors.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, receive, use, store, disclose, retain and protect personal information when you access our website, submit a healthcare request, make an appointment, communicate with our team or use any of our healthcare coordination services.

As a healthcare coordination and concierge services company, we recognise that some of the information shared with us may relate to an individual’s physical or mental health. Such information requires particular care and appropriate safeguards.

We process personal information in accordance with applicable laws and regulatory requirements, including the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) of the Republic of Ghana.

By using our website or voluntarily submitting information through our communication channels, you acknowledge that you have been informed of the practices described in this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required as a lawful basis for processing, we will seek that consent appropriately.

2. Who We Are

MEDs Specialists Connect® is a healthcare coordination and concierge services company facilitating access to healthcare professionals, specialists, hospitals, clinics, diagnostic centres and other healthcare-related services.

For purposes of applicable data protection legislation, MEDs determines the purposes and means of processing personal information collected through its website and operational channels, and acts as a data controller in respect of such processing.

3. Personal Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on the nature of your interaction with MEDs Specialists Connect and the services you request.

3.1 Personal Identification Information

We may collect:

  • Full name.
  • Telephone and mobile numbers.
  • Email address.
  • Residential or service location.
  • Organisation or institutional affiliation, where applicable.
  • Other identifying information voluntarily supplied through our forms or communication channels.

3.2 Healthcare and Medical-Related Information

Where necessary to facilitate healthcare services, we may collect limited health-related information, including:

  • The nature of the medical assistance requested.
  • The medical specialty or healthcare service required.
  • The identity of the individual requiring assistance.
  • The urgency of the request.
  • Relevant medical information voluntarily provided.
  • Additional information necessary to coordinate an appointment, referral or healthcare service.

Health information is treated as sensitive personal information and is accessed only where necessary for legitimate healthcare coordination and related operational purposes.

We encourage users to provide only the information reasonably necessary to process their requests.

Important: Please do not submit complete medical records, medical reports, laboratory results, identification documents or other highly sensitive records through general website enquiry forms unless MEDs has specifically provided an appropriate and secure channel for that purpose.

3.3 Communication Information

When you contact us through telephone calls, email, WhatsApp or other communication channels, we may receive and retain information contained in your communications.

This may include:

  • Enquiries and service requests.
  • Appointment-related discussions.
  • Referral information.
  • Customer support communications.
  • Feedback and complaints.
  • Other information voluntarily provided during interactions with our team.

3.4 Transaction and Payment Information

Where you make payments for services arranged through MEDs, we may maintain transaction-related information, including:

  • Invoice references.
  • Payment amounts.
  • Payment status.
  • Transaction references.
  • Payment dates.
  • Receipts and related financial records.

Online payments are processed through Paystack.

We do not collect or store your complete payment card credentials or Mobile Money PIN through our website. Such sensitive payment authentication information is handled through the payment service provider.

3.5 Technical and Website Information

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be processed automatically to support website delivery, functionality and security.

This may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device and operating system information.
  • Technical connection information.
  • Security and server-related logs.

Our website hosting and security infrastructure may process this information as part of normal website operations.

4. How We Collect Your Information

We collect personal information through the following channels:

  1. Website Forms: When you submit healthcare requests, appointment enquiries, referral information or general messages through our website.
  2. Telephone: When you contact our customer support or service coordination team.
  3. Email: When you communicate with us electronically.
  4. WhatsApp: When you initiate or participate in conversations through our official WhatsApp communication channels.
  5. Healthcare Referrals: When a doctor, hospital, clinic or other authorised party refers an individual to MEDs.
  6. Payment Platforms: When transaction information is generated through payments processed by Paystack.
  7. Website Infrastructure: Through technical information generated when you access our website.

Where information relates to another individual, the person submitting the information should have the appropriate authority or lawful basis to provide it.

5. Purposes for Which We Use Your Information

MEDs Specialists Connect collects and processes personal information only for specified and legitimate purposes.

These purposes include:

5.1 Healthcare Coordination

To understand your healthcare needs and facilitate access to appropriate medical professionals, specialists, hospitals, diagnostic centres and other healthcare providers.

5.2 Appointment and Service Management

To arrange consultations, confirm appointments, coordinate home healthcare services and communicate relevant service information.

5.3 Referral Management

To receive, assess and coordinate referrals from doctors, hospitals, clinics and other healthcare institutions.

5.4 Customer Communication

To respond to enquiries, provide updates, confirm appointments, communicate changes and address customer concerns.

5.5 Payment and Financial Administration

To generate invoices, verify payments, issue receipts, manage refunds and maintain appropriate transaction records.

5.6 Operational and Administrative Management

To manage our business operations, maintain service records, improve customer support and monitor the effectiveness of our healthcare coordination processes.

5.7 Security and Fraud Prevention

To protect our website, digital infrastructure, users and operational systems against unauthorised access, misuse, fraudulent activities and security threats.

5.8 Legal and Regulatory Compliance

To comply with applicable laws, regulatory requirements, lawful requests from competent authorities and other legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information to third parties and do not use information collected through our healthcare request forms for targeted advertising or unrelated marketing purposes.

6. Lawful Basis For Processing

MEDs processes personal information in accordance with the lawful grounds recognised under applicable data protection legislation.

Depending on the nature of the information and the circumstances of processing, these grounds may include:

  • Consent: Where you voluntarily authorise us to collect, use or share your information for a specified purpose.
  • Contractual Necessity: Where processing is necessary to respond to a service request or perform an agreed service arrangement.
  • Legal Obligations: Where processing is required to comply with applicable laws and regulatory requirements.
  • Vital Interests: Where processing is necessary to protect an individual’s life or other vital interests in circumstances permitted by law.
  • Other Lawful Grounds: Where processing is otherwise authorised under applicable data protection legislation.

Health information constitutes sensitive personal data under Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

We will process such information only where a lawful condition permits the processing and appropriate safeguards are applied.

Where consent is relied upon, it must be obtained in accordance with applicable legal requirements. You may withdraw consent at any time, subject to any other lawful basis for retaining or processing the information.

7. Sharing and Disclosure of Personal Information

MEDs Specialists Connect respects the confidentiality of personal information.

We do not sell, rent or commercially trade users’ personal information.

However, certain information may need to be disclosed to authorised parties where necessary to facilitate our services or comply with legal obligations.

7.1 Healthcare Professionals and Institutions

Where you request healthcare assistance, we may share relevant information with:

  • Medical specialists and doctors.
  • Hospitals and clinics.
  • Diagnostic and laboratory centres.
  • Nursing and home healthcare professionals.
  • Other relevant healthcare service providers.

Information is shared only to the extent reasonably necessary to facilitate the requested service and in accordance with the applicable lawful basis and confidentiality requirements.

7.2 Technology and Service Providers

We engage selected third-party service providers to support our website, communications, payments and operational infrastructure.

These providers may process limited personal or technical information on our behalf.

They include:

Web3Forms – Form Delivery

Website form submissions are transmitted through Web3Forms to our designated email inbox for processing and response.

Paystack – Payment Processing

Paystack facilitates online payments for invoices and related services. Payment authentication information is handled through its payment infrastructure.

Cloudflare – Website Hosting and Security

Cloudflare supports website delivery, infrastructure performance and security. Technical information, including IP addresses, may be processed as part of these functions.

Email Service Providers

Our email service providers facilitate the delivery of invoices, receipts, service notifications and other communications.

WhatsApp

Where you choose to communicate with us through WhatsApp, your communications are also subject to WhatsApp’s applicable privacy policy and terms.

Mapping Services

Where you access mapping links from our website, the relevant mapping provider may process technical and location-related information under its own privacy practices.

We expect relevant service providers to apply appropriate confidentiality and security measures in accordance with their contractual obligations and applicable law.

7.3 Legal and Regulatory Authorities

We may disclose personal information where required or authorised by law, including in response to lawful requests from courts, regulatory authorities, law enforcement agencies or other competent public institutions.

7.4 Protection of Rights and Security

We may disclose relevant information where reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, protect the rights and safety of individuals, enforce our legal agreements or address security incidents, subject to applicable legal requirements.

8. Third-Party Services and International Data Transfers

Certain technology providers engaged by MEDs may operate infrastructure or process information in jurisdictions outside Ghana.

Consequently, information submitted through our website or communication channels may be processed or stored outside Ghana, depending on the location and infrastructure of the relevant service provider.

Where personal information is transferred outside Ghana, MEDs will take reasonable steps to ensure that the transfer and subsequent processing comply with applicable data protection requirements and that appropriate safeguards are maintained.

Third-party platforms such as Paystack, WhatsApp, Cloudflare and mapping services operate independently and may have their own privacy policies, security arrangements and data processing practices.

MEDs does not control all aspects of these external platforms.

Users are encouraged to review the privacy policies of relevant third-party services before using them.

9. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

MEDs Specialists Connect aims to maintain a privacy-conscious website experience.

Our website does not currently use advertising or behavioural tracking cookies.

The website may store your preferred display setting, such as light or dark mode, on your device to improve your browsing experience.

This preference is not used to build advertising profiles or track your browsing activities across unrelated websites.

Our website hosting and security infrastructure may independently process technical information necessary for website delivery, protection and performance.

If additional cookies, analytics tools or tracking technologies are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

MEDs retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, comply with legal obligations, maintain appropriate business records and address legitimate operational requirements.

Retention periods may vary depending on:

  • The nature of the information.
  • The type of service requested.
  • Whether a healthcare service or referral has been completed.
  • Applicable financial and regulatory recordkeeping requirements.
  • The existence of ongoing disputes or legal obligations.
  • Relevant professional and contractual obligations.

When personal information is no longer required, MEDs will take reasonable steps to securely delete, destroy, anonymise or otherwise remove identifying information, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

Users may request the deletion of their personal information, subject to lawful grounds for continued retention.

11. Data Security and Protection Measures

MEDs Specialists Connect recognises that protecting personal and health information is essential to maintaining user confidence.

We implement technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, destruction or loss.

Our current website and operational security measures include:

11.1 Encrypted Website Communications

Our website uses HTTPS encryption to protect information transmitted between users’ browsers and our website infrastructure.

11.2 Restricted Staff Access

Access to internal systems is restricted to authorised personnel based on their assigned responsibilities.

11.3 Authentication and Access Controls

Our internal systems use individual staff accounts and additional authentication measures, including authenticator-based verification, to strengthen account security.

11.4 Role-Based Permissions

Access to information is determined by staff roles and operational responsibilities. Personnel who do not require access to sensitive healthcare information should not have access to such information.

11.5 Access Monitoring

We maintain system records that support the monitoring of access to and changes made to relevant records.

11.6 Session Management

Our systems use automatic session sign-out measures to reduce the risk of unauthorised access through unattended sessions.

11.7 Data Minimisation in Internal Communications

Internal notifications are designed to minimise the disclosure of personal information. For example, new-request notifications may use case references and service categories rather than including a patient’s name, telephone number or detailed health information.

11.8 Secure Transaction and Tracking References

Where invoice and tracking links are provided, we use measures intended to prevent unauthorised access and avoid exposing sensitive health information through those links.

11.9 Incident Response

Where a personal data security incident occurs, MEDs will investigate, assess the potential impact, take appropriate remedial measures and make notifications to affected individuals and the Data Protection Commission where required by applicable law.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. Although we take reasonable precautions, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Users should avoid transmitting highly sensitive medical documents through ordinary, unsecured communication channels.

12. Personal Data Breaches

A personal data breach may involve the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of or access to personal information.

If MEDs becomes aware of a suspected or confirmed breach affecting personal information, we will:

  1. Investigate the incident promptly.
  2. Assess the nature and extent of the information affected.
  3. Take reasonable steps to contain the incident and prevent further exposure.
  4. Implement appropriate corrective and security measures.
  5. Notify the Data Protection Commission and affected individuals where required by applicable law.
  6. Maintain appropriate records of the incident and the actions taken.

We recognise the importance of timely breach reporting and will comply with applicable notification requirements.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Under Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), individuals have rights concerning the personal information held and processed about them.

Subject to applicable legal conditions and limitations, these rights include:

13.1 Right to Be Informed

You have the right to understand what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it and the parties with whom it may be shared.

13.2 Right of Access

You may request information about the personal data MEDs holds about you and the purposes for which it is processed.

13.3 Right to Rectification

You may request the correction of inaccurate, incomplete or outdated personal information.

13.4 Right to Prevent or Object to Processing

You may request that we stop or restrict particular processing activities where the conditions under applicable law are satisfied.

13.5 Right to Withdraw Consent

Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time.

Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing undertaken before the withdrawal.

It also does not prevent processing where another lawful basis permits us to continue.

13.6 Right to Request Deletion

You may request the deletion of personal information where applicable legal conditions permit such deletion.

However, we may retain certain information where required by law or necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

13.7 Right to Complain

You have the right to raise concerns about how your personal information is collected, used, disclosed or protected.

You may contact MEDs directly or submit a complaint to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.

13.8 Right Relating to Automated Decisions

Where applicable, you may exercise your rights concerning decisions significantly affecting you that are based solely on automated processing, in accordance with applicable law.

15. Children’s Personal Information

MEDs Specialists Connect recognises the importance of protecting children’s personal information.

Where a healthcare request concerns a child or an individual who is legally incapable of providing the necessary consent, information should be submitted by a parent, guardian or other person legally authorised to act on their behalf.

We will handle such information with appropriate care and in accordance with applicable legal requirements governing children’s personal data and sensitive personal information.

We do not knowingly seek to collect children’s personal information for unrelated commercial or advertising purposes.

16. Confidentiality of Health Information

MEDs treats health-related information as confidential and applies additional safeguards to its handling.

Information relating to a patient’s medical condition, requested treatment, healthcare referral or consultation is shared only where necessary for the coordination of the relevant service and where a lawful basis permits such disclosure.

Healthcare professionals and institutions receiving information through our coordination services are expected to maintain appropriate professional confidentiality and comply with their applicable professional and legal obligations.

Users should understand that once information is shared with an independent healthcare institution or professional, that provider may also process the information under its own privacy and professional confidentiality obligations.

MEDs does not control the entirety of the independent records management practices of third-party healthcare institutions.

These may include payment portals, WhatsApp, mapping services, healthcare institutions and other third-party resources.

When you follow an external link, you leave the MEDs website and become subject to the privacy practices and terms of the relevant third-party service.
