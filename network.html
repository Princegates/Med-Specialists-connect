Our healthcare network

The doctors, hospitals and diagnostic centres we work with, and how we choose them.

Who is in the network

We connect patients with private providers across Accra and beyond. Each one is checked before we propose them to anyone.

  • Specialist doctors

    Consultants and specialists in private practice or based at a clinic or hospital.

  • Hospitals and clinics

    Facilities that can receive patients we refer and handle admissions.

  • Diagnostic centres

    Laboratories and imaging centres for tests and scans.

  • Home and transport care

    Home nursing, doctor home visits and ambulance providers.

How we choose

  • We check each provider’s registration or licence before they are used.
  • We keep track of when licences and credentials expire, and stop using a provider whose credentials have lapsed.
  • We tell you the cost before you pay, and show our coordination fee separately from the provider’s charge.
  • We list a provider here only with their consent.
