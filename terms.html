Terms of Service

Last updated: 24 November 2026

These terms apply when you use the MEDs Specialists Connect website or ask us to arrange a service. By sending a request or paying an invoice, you agree to them. Please read them together with our Privacy Policy and our Medical disclaimer.

Who we are

MEDs Specialists Connect is a healthcare operations and concierge company based at 6 Worship Curve, Spintex Road, Accra, Ghana. You can reach us on 0243 560 080 or at info@medspecialistconnect.com.

What we do

We connect you with specialists, clinics, laboratories and other providers, arrange appointments, home or private consultations and on-call services, and coordinate your care. We are not a hospital, an emergency service or an insurer. The doctors, nurses, laboratory staff and facilities we arrange are responsible for the care and advice they give, and clinical decisions are always theirs.

Not medical advice, and emergencies

This website gives general information only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. In an emergency, call 112 or go to the nearest hospital straight away. Do not use the request form or WhatsApp for emergencies.

Requests and appointments

Please give accurate information, including the right phone number so we can reach you. Sending a request does not guarantee an appointment, a particular specialist or a particular time. Availability depends on the provider. For private or home consultations, you tell us which one you want when you ask.

Fees and invoices

We do not show prices on the website. After you ask for a service, we send you an invoice that shows what you will pay, before you pay anything. For some services we first confirm the cost with you and then send the invoice. All amounts are in Ghana cedis (GH₵).

For private and home consultations, the invoice shows the service fee, nursing care and the laboratory technician as separate lines. Laboratory tests, follow-up care and any other service your doctor recommends are arranged with you and charged separately, and we tell you before you pay for them.

Paying

You pay an invoice online through Paystack, our secure payment partner, by Mobile Money, card or bank transfer. You enter your card or Mobile Money details only on Paystack’s page. We never see or store them. When Paystack confirms your payment, we mark the invoice as paid and send you a receipt. Your payment confirms your request.

Changes, cancellations and refunds

  • Tell us as soon as you can if you want to cancel or change a visit. Call 0243 560 080 or email info@medspecialistconnect.com.
  • If we cannot arrange the service you paid for, we refund the full amount.
  • If you cancel before we have arranged a provider for you, we refund the full amount.
  • If a provider has already been arranged or the visit has taken place, the fee may not be refundable. We will explain this when you ask to cancel.
  • Refunds go back to the way you paid, through Paystack. The time it takes depends on your bank or Mobile Money provider.

Your part

Please be available at the agreed time, tell the clinician the truth about your health, and treat our staff and providers with respect. We may refuse or stop a service if someone is abusive or gives false information.

Your information

We use your information to deal with your request, as described in our Privacy Policy. By sending a request you agree that we may call you about it and share what is needed with the provider we arrange for you.

Our responsibility

We take reasonable care in choosing and coordinating providers, and we check that clinics and hospitals hold the licences they need. We are not responsible for the clinical care or advice a provider gives. We are not responsible for delays or losses outside our control, such as a provider cancelling, traffic, or network failures. Nothing in these terms limits any right you have under Ghanaian law that cannot be limited.

This website

Do not misuse the website or try to interfere with it, or submit false or spam requests. We may block anyone who does. Links to other websites and services, such as WhatsApp and maps, are controlled by their owners.

Changes and governing law

We may update these terms, and the date at the top shows when they last changed. The laws of Ghana apply to these terms. If you have a complaint, please contact us first at info@medspecialistconnect.com and we will try to put it right.
