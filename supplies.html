Medical consumables

We help hospitals and clinics source specialist cardiac and vascular products. Availability depends on supply and on local regulatory requirements.

Cardiac products

  • Pacemakers (single and dual chamber)
  • Ablation products (surgical)
  • Aortic stent grafts
  • Blood management and diagnostics
  • Cannulae
  • Cardiopulmonary products
  • Electrosurgical products
  • Extracorporeal life support (ECLS)
  • Heart valve replacement
  • Pediatric perfusion products
  • Revascularization products (surgical)

Vascular products

Coronary

  • Balloons
  • Catheters
  • Guidewires and accessories
  • Stents

Peripheral vascular

  • Arteriovenous fistula (AVF) creation
  • Chronic total occlusion devices
  • Deep venous
  • Directional atherectomy systems
  • Drug-coated balloons
  • Embolic protection devices
  • Guidewires
  • Infusion therapy products
  • Intraprocedural monitoring
  • Peripheral and biliary stents
  • Peripheral embolization
  • PTA balloons
  • Snares
  • Superficial vein products
  • Support catheters

Medical accessories

The accessories that go with your consumables and equipment.

Tell us which accessories your unit needs, for example guidewires and accessories for coronary procedures, and we will source them and send you a quotation.

Availability depends on supply and on local regulatory requirements.

Medical equipment

We provide the deployment and management of cutting-edge medical equipment for specialized units, as part of the end-to-end operational support we give hospitals and clinics.

We manage the equipment, day-to-day operations and administrative services of the units we run, for example the Dialysis Department at ADMed Specialist Clinic.

Looking for a particular piece of equipment for your facility? Tell us what you need, and we will come back to you with options and a quotation.

Request a quotation

Tell us what your unit needs, with product names and quantities if you have them. We will reply with availability and a price.

Please do not include patient names or records.
